# Project 07 — Scenario map

**Who this is for:** anyone who wants to see, for a common IT operations
scenario, what I did in real work, where the evidence is in this repository and
what this lab shows.

How to read the table:

- **What I did in real work** lists only my work at TikTok (IT Operations
  Engineer, Dublin, EMEA, March 2023 – September 2026). A dash (—) means I did
  not do this in real work. Tools I did not administer are marked
  **new to me — lab or design only**.
- **Evidence in this repo** links to files in this repository.
- **What the lab shows** is the Jira Service Management lab in this project.
  Everything in the lab is synthetic.
- Items marked **Step 15** are documented in [build log, Step 15](build-log.md#step-15): country queues with
  auto-assignment, laptop back to *In use* when the request is resolved, and a
  SaaS / external access request with approval.
