# Project 07 — User guide: getting IT help

**Who this is for:** employees who need help from IT.
**What you can do after reading:** raise the right request, find your laptop
asset tag, report a laptop problem and return your laptop when you leave.

> This guide belongs to a lab. The service desk, the people and the laptops are
> synthetic. It shows how the lab portal works, not a real company's process.

---

## Before you start

- You need an account that can open the IT customer portal.
- For a laptop problem, have the laptop with you, or know its asset tag or
  serial number.

---

## 1. Search the help articles first

1. Open the IT customer portal.
2. Type your problem in the search box, for example *password* or *VPN*.
3. Read the article under **Self-help resources** if one appears.
4. If the steps fix your problem, you are done. If not, raise a request.

Articles in the IT Help Centre:

- How to reset your password
- How to connect to the VPN when working remotely
- My laptop is slow - first steps
- How to find your laptop asset tag and report a problem
- How to return your laptop when you leave

---

## 2. Raise a request

The portal has two groups. Choose the one that matches what you need.

| You need | Portal group | Request type |
|---|---|---|
| Accounts and access for a new person | Accounts & access | New starter (onboarding) |
| Close accounts and return equipment for a person who leaves | Accounts & access | Leaver (offboarding) |
| Access to an app or admin rights | Accounts & access | Access request (apps & admin rights) |
| Help with a broken or faulty laptop or device | Hardware & devices | Laptop or device issue |

1. Open the portal group.
2. Choose the request type.
3. Fill every required field.
4. Select **Send**.

What happens next:

- You get a reference number, for example `IT-13`. Keep it.
- An access request goes to an approver first. Nobody can approve their own
  request. IT starts work only after approval.
- If IT asks you a question, the request waits for you. Reply in the request
  so IT can continue.

---

## 3. Find your laptop asset tag

Every laptop has an asset tag that starts with `LT-`, for example `LT-0001`.

1. Look for the IT asset tag on your laptop.
2. If you cannot find it, use the serial number instead. The laptop picker in
   the portal accepts both.

---

## 4. Report a laptop problem

1. Open **Hardware & devices**.
2. Choose **Laptop or device issue**.
3. In **Which laptop?**, type your asset tag or serial number.
4. Pick your laptop from the list. You must pick one before you can send.
5. Describe the problem.
6. Select **Send**.

What happens next: IT sees your laptop's serial number, model and owner on the
request. The laptop's status changes to **In repair** in the IT inventory.

---

## 5. Return your laptop when you leave

Your manager raises the **Leaver (offboarding)** request. You do not raise it
yourself.

**Before your last day:**

1. Save your work to OneDrive or SharePoint.
2. Sign out of your personal accounts on the laptop.
3. Do not wipe the laptop yourself. IT does this.

**On your last day:**

1. Bring the laptop and its charger to IT.
2. If you work remotely, use the courier label IT gives you.

**What IT does:**

1. Checks the asset tag and the condition of the laptop.
2. Confirms the return in the Leaver request.
3. Wipes and checks the laptop, then puts it back in stock.
