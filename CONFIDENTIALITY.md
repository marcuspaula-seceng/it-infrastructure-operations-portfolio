# Confidentiality and Sanitisation Policy

This repository is a public professional portfolio. It is written specifically
to avoid disclosing any confidential, proprietary or employer-specific
information.

## What this repository deliberately excludes

- Employee, colleague or manager names
- Internal room, floor or building names
- Internal hostnames, domains, URLs or IP addresses
- Real asset tags, serial numbers or ticket references
- Screenshots or copies of internal documents
- Credentials, tokens, keys or access details
- Internal network topology or addressing schemes
- Employer logos, branding or proprietary materials

## Nature of the published artefacts

The case studies describe genuine professional work at a **high level**. The
accompanying scripts, checklists, diagrams and datasets are **sanitised
reference implementations**, created independently for portfolio purposes.

- They are **not** the original enterprise production tooling.
- They do **not** reproduce internal naming conventions, formats or topology.
- All datasets are **entirely synthetic** and were generated for demonstration.

## Contribution accuracy

Contributions are described honestly. Where work was collaborative or supporting
rather than sole ownership, the wording reflects that. In particular, the asset
audit application referenced in Project 02 was **developed by a colleague**;
this portfolio only publishes an independent demonstration tool built to
illustrate the reconciliation principle.

## Reporting a concern

If you believe any content here inadvertently discloses sensitive information,
please open an issue or contact the maintainer, and it will be reviewed and
removed promptly. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
